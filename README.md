# Info

RtD Oceans & Cuisine Expansion transforms the oceans into a living ecosystem while expanding every biome with unique crops, regional dishes, sea creatures, predators, relic progression, and a powerful ocean boss encounter.

Every biome now has identity through food, farming, and sea life.

* All wild vegetables can be replanted and farmed
* All farming crops have a 75% chance to drop seeds for replanting
* All relics are now dropped by the new Belzor, can you survive his wrath?

# Translations

* Currently English, Chinese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Russian, Spanish, & Ukrainian are supported.
* Is your language not listed? Join my discord and feel free to make request. 
* Want to make your own JSON? Place it inside the mods plugin `Asset` Folder.

## OCEAN LIFE

### Friendly Creatures

* Dolphin – Instantly tamed using Shrimp
* Orca – Quickly tamed using sea animal meat
* Mermaid – Rare tameable ocean entity

### Passive Sea Animals (Flee Behavior)

* Cod – Black Forest & Ocean
* Tuna – Black Forest & Ocean
* Squid – Swamp & Ocean
* Manta – Swamp & Ocean
* Marlin – Plains & Ocean
* Turtle – Mistlands & Ocean
* Sperm Whale – Ocean

The smallest whale variant begins at 1,000 HP.
